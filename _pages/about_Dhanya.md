Growing up near the tropical rainforests of the Western Ghats in southern India, my fascination with the natural world blossomed early. Following an undergraduate degree in Biology from the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru, I pursued the MEME master’s programme in Evolutionary Biology. Broadly, my research interests lie in understanding biodiversity responses to changing environments. For my PhD, I am exploring how ecological communities can adapt and stay resilient in a rapidly transforming world. Passionate about making science fun, accessible and inclusive, I can otherwise be found experimenting with vegan recipes, humming earworms or in a surprisingly meaningful conversation with my plants.
