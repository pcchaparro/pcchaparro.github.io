### Vanessa Cunha

#### Postodoc

What mainly interests me is how environmental change shapes phenotypic variation and evolutionary dynamics within and between species. I am particularly interested in how organisms respond to environmental stress through phenotypic plasticity and adaptation, and how these responses influence ecological interactions and community dynamics. Currently, I investigate how the rate of environmental change shapes the contribution of adaptation to ecological resilience across demographic, population, and community levels, and identify the genomic and phenotypic mechanisms underlying these responses.
